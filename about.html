ABOUT US

WHO IS 360 BOYZ SERCUE

360 Boyz Secure was incorporated in South Africa on 3 May 2019. Foundered by Preshu Singh the company initially established, its roots on Smith Street in Dundee before growing its local presence. Over the years, the firm expanded from a local security outfit into a broader vendor of technical services, earning an AA Qualified approved technical services member with operations at 4 Avon Road in Dundee.

The company grew its reputation by addressing multi-dimensional protection needs in the Umzinyathi District. They diversified their traditional physical guarding option into comprehensive tactical operations and electronic surveillance installation.

Our Mission

360 Boyz Secures mission is to provide high-quality, affordable, and reliable security services. The company aims to give clients peace of mind by offering different security solutions, including VIP protection, security guarding , CCTV installation, and private investigations.

Our Vision

360 Boyz Secures vision is to be a trusted , all-round security company that protects people, businesses, and communities. The company aims to provide professional security services while also working with local communities to help prevent crime and create a safer environment across KwaZulu-Natal.

Why Choose Us

360 Boyz Secure is dedicated to providing dependable security solutions that meet the needs of clients. We focus on protecting people and property while building strong relationships with the communities we serve.
